About

Every street has someone who knows where the water goes. Mr Rogers keeps that knowledge when they move away.

Your privacy

• Your address and real name are never shown
• Notes show a rough distance band, never metres
• Photo location data is removed

Who counts as local

Notes from people living nearby rank higher. Notes from further away need a moderator's OK and are labelled.

Not official advice

Check council flood information for your property. Emergency? Call 000.
